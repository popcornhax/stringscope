How to see the hidden 3D shape

  1. Go fullscreen and sit about arm's length from the screen.
  2. Wall-eyed (default): look through the screen, as if at something far behind it. The two guide dots at the top will drift into three or four — relax until there are exactly three.
  3. Keep that focus and let your gaze wander down into the lines. The shape snaps into depth; once it's locked you can look around it.
  4. Cross-eyed: switch the viewing mode, then cross your eyes slightly (try focusing on a fingertip held halfway to the screen) until the dots make three.

Tips: start with Still motion, a larger eye separation if fusion feels strained on a big screen, and a smaller one on a laptop. Moving models stay fused; the line design itself never reshuffles — only the strings near the object bend.
